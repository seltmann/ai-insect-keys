# Key to candidate Anthidium species from coastal Santa Barbara County, California

Last updated: 2026-07-31

## Scope and source

This is a regional, reduced key for the `Anthidium` species treated in the
coastal Santa Barbara County checklist:

- Primary local candidates: `A. palliventre`, `A. maculosum`, `A. illustre`,
  `A. tenuiflorae`, `A. mormonum`, `A. utahense`, `A. edwardsii`,
  `A. palmarum`, and `A. collectum`.
- Range-overlap watchlist species from the full Gonzalez and Griswold (2013)
  distribution scan: `A. atrifrons`, `A. clypeodentatum`, `A. dammersi`,
  `A. jocosum`, `A. pallidiclypeum`, and `A. placitum`.

The couplets below are adapted from the North American female and male keys and
species diagnoses in Gonzalez and Griswold (2013). They are intended for adult
specimens examined under magnification. Several characters are difficult or
unreliable from photographs alone.

Use the full paper and figures for final determinations. Local reference file
(ignored by Git): `references/Gonzalez-Griswold-2013-Anthidium-Western-Hemisphere-key.pdf`.

Abbreviations follow the paper: `T1`, `T2`, etc. are metasomal terga, and `S4`,
`S6`, etc. are metasomal sterna. "Maculations" are yellow to cream integumental
marks. "Watchlist" means range-overlap only in the current checklist, not a
confirmed Santa Barbara County species-level record.

## IDtools confirmation checks

These notes summarize endpoint checks from the `Diagnostic characteristics` and
`May be confused with` sections in the `Exotic Bee ID` `Anthidium` fact sheets
and from the IDtools North American `Anthidium` key. Use them after reaching a
species endpoint in the regional key. Most male checks require sterna or
terminalia, and most female checks require a clear view of the clypeus, labrum,
basitarsi, hind tibia, propodeal triangle, and `T6`.

- `Anthidium atrifrons` (watchlist): Confirm females by the entirely black,
  strongly convex clypeus, dense tomentum on the fore basitarsus, and `T6` with
  a median emargination plus a depressed apical rim that fades before the
  lateral angle. Confirm males by the broad black `S4` brush, `S6` lateral
  projections with sublateral carinae, and a rounded to subtriangular `T7`
  lateral lobe. Compare pale females with `A. tenuiflorae` and `A. collectum`,
  and males with `A. dammersi`.
- `Anthidium clypeodentatum` (watchlist): Confirm females by the short white
  tomentum on the posterior surface of the scape, a strongly toothed or
  tuberculate clypeal margin, seven-toothed mandibles, and `T6` with a narrow
  midapical projection but no lateral projection. Confirm males by `S8` without
  a long apical process, `S6` with a small triangular median lobe, and thick
  reddish-brown hairs on `S4`. The closest IDtools comparison is
  `A. psoraleae`.
- `Anthidium collectum`: Confirm females by the slightly wavy, thin clypeal
  margin, long curved labral preapical projections, shiny to weakly lineolate
  propodeal triangle, tomentose basitarsi, and `T6` lacking a lateral angle.
  Confirm males by the broad short black `S4` brush, small angulate `S6`
  lateral lobes, apically notched `S6` median lobe, rounded `T7` lateral lobe,
  and `S8` bent strongly in profile. Compare females with `A. labergei` and
  males with `A. schwarzi`.
- `Anthidium dammersi` (watchlist): Confirm females by the white scopa, weakly
  shiny to dull terga, thin wavy clypeal margin, and `T6` with a truncate distal
  margin and depressed apical rim visible across the margin, without lateral
  spines or a preapical carina. Confirm males by the broad short black `S4`
  brush, `S6` with an acute lateral lobe and emarginate median lobe, and `T7`
  with the lateral lobe broad basally and tapering apically. Compare males
  especially with `A. atrifrons`.
- `Anthidium edwardsii`: Confirm females by five-toothed mandibles, a dull
  finely lineolate propodeal triangle, lack of dense basitarsal tomentum, and
  `T6` without a lateral angle but slightly swollen preapically, with a narrow
  depressed rim and crenulate preapical carina. Confirm males by the narrow,
  pointed to subtriangular `T7` lateral lobe, reddish-brown `S4` brush, and
  `S6` with small lateral spines and a rectangular median lobe. Compare most
  carefully with `A. placitum`.
- `Anthidium illustre`: Confirm females by large body size, six- to
  eight-toothed mandibles, labral preapical projections, and bilobed `T6` with
  a deep median emargination and lateral spines. Confirm males by the absence
  of a median `S4` brush, the concave `S4` margin with lateral hair tufts,
  obliquely truncate `S6` lateral lobes, pointed `S7` and `S8`, and `T6`
  lateral spine longer than the `T7` median spine. The main IDtools comparison
  is `A. formosum`.
- `Anthidium jocosum` (watchlist): Confirm females by small body size, smooth
  shiny propodeal triangle, sparse basitarsal tomentum, `T6` with the depressed
  apical rim visible across the whole distal margin, and no yellow maculations
  on the clypeus or paraocular area. Confirm males by a sparse reddish-brown
  `S4` brush, blunt `S6` lateral lobes, a broad rounded `S6` median lobe, and a
  subquadrate `T7` lateral lobe about twice as broad as the gap to the median
  spine. Compare with `A. utahense`.
- `Anthidium maculosum`: Confirm both sexes by the distinct anterior carina on
  the hind tibia and dull frons with coarse sparse punctures. Females also have
  a labrum without basal protuberances or preapical projections and `T6` with a
  crenulate preapical carina plus a small acute lateral projection. Males lack
  an `S4` brush and have a long digitiform `T7` lateral lobe, a short truncate
  `S8` process, and a curved stout `T6` lateral spine.
- `Anthidium mormonum`: Confirm females by a distinctly dull propodeal triangle,
  no hind-tibial carina, no dense tomentum on the outer basitarsi, strongly
  elevated tergal discs, and `T6` with a depressed apical rim projected
  medially. Confirm males by an apically rounded `T7` lateral lobe, acute `S6`
  lateral lobes, a broad rounded `S6` median lobe, and a narrow sparse
  reddish-brown `S4` brush that may be hard to see. Compare small specimens
  with `A. utahense`.
- `Anthidium pallidiclypeum` (watchlist): Confirm females by the concave
  clypeal apex lacking strong sublateral projections, weakly shiny to finely
  lineolate propodeal triangle, and `T6` without lateral spines. Confirm males
  by a sparse reddish-brown `S4` brush, `S6` lacking lateral lobes and having a
  broad truncate to slightly rounded median lobe, rounded `T7` lateral lobes,
  and a long bifid `S8` process with enlarged or clubbed ends. Compare with
  `A. edwardsii` and `A. placitum`.
- `Anthidium palliventre`: Confirm females by the long fringe of hairs on the
  posterior margin of the fore basitarsus, which IDtools treats as separating
  it from all other U.S. `Anthidium`, plus a six-toothed mandible and `T6` with
  distinct lateral angles. Confirm males by the curved tapering `T7` lateral
  lobe, acute ventrally directed `S6` lateral lobes, bidentate `S6` median lobe,
  and bifid `S8`. The sand-dune nesting association is also a useful locality
  check.
- `Anthidium palmarum`: Confirm females by rounded sublateral lobes on `T6`,
  dense basitarsal tomentum, weakly shiny lineolate to imbricate propodeal
  triangle, and four- to five-toothed mandibles. Confirm males by the wide brush
  of long black hairs on `S4`, medially concave `S4` margin, low rounded `S6`
  lateral lobes, short emarginate `S6` median lobe, curved tapering `T7` lateral
  lobe, and weakly bifid `S8`. Compare with `A. schwarzi` outside the current
  regional list.
- `Anthidium placitum` (watchlist): Confirm females by large size, simple
  hooked or wavy clypeal hairs, dense basitarsal tomentum, doubly carinate
  narrow tergal margins, and `T6` with a blunt laterally projected tooth.
  Confirm males by a broad rounded `T7` lateral lobe nearly twice as broad as
  the gap to the median spine, medially directed `S6` lateral lobes, a
  subtriangular `S6` median lobe, a narrow reddish `S4` brush, and a long narrow
  bifid `S8` process. Compare with `A. edwardsii`.
- `Anthidium tenuiflorae`: Confirm females by dense tomentum on the fore and
  mid basitarsi, pale scopa, shiny terga, narrow smooth shiny apical tergal
  borders, and `T6` broadly truncate without a distinct median emargination or
  preapical carina. Confirm males by a long black `S4` brush along about
  one-third of the margin, acute `S6` lateral lobes with slightly concave outer
  margins, a broad rounded `S6` median lobe, broad bifid `S8`, and a broad
  rounded `T7` lateral lobe. Compare pale females with `A. atrifrons`.
- `Anthidium utahense`: Confirm females by sparse tomentum on the outer
  basitarsi, a weakly lineolate to smooth and shiny propodeal triangle, yellow
  clypeal and paraocular maculations, and `T6` with small lateral angles and a
  broad truncate margin. Confirm males by a narrow dense reddish-brown `S4`
  brush, acute dark `S6` lateral lobes, a broad rounded `S6` median lobe, long
  narrow bifid `S8`, and a subquadrate `T7` lateral lobe about twice as broad
  as the gap to the median spine. Compare with `A. jocosum` and small
  `A. mormonum`.

## Key to females

1. `T6` with a deep median emargination on the distal margin, roughly one-fifth
   the width of the tergum; preapical carina absent; large, heavily maculated
   bee, usually more than 11 mm. -> `Anthidium illustre`

1'. `T6` without a deep median emargination; notch small, weak, or absent. -> 2

2. Hind tibia with a distinct longitudinal carina on the anterior margin; frons
   dull and sparsely punctate, with simple curly or hooked hairs; `T6` with a
   distinct acute lateral projection. -> `Anthidium maculosum`

2'. Hind tibia without a distinct longitudinal carina, or carina weak and not
    accompanied by the dull, sparse frons above. -> 3

3. Clypeus with an evident median V-shaped or angled emargination on the distal
   margin; clypeal disc mostly with simple curly, hooked, or wavy hairs. -> 4

3'. Clypeus without a clear median V-shaped emargination, though the margin may
    be gently concave or wavy. -> 6

4. Clypeus without acute sublateral teeth on the distal margin; clypeus weakly
   to moderately convex, with lateral tubercles not strongly projected;
   depressed marginal zone of `T5` sparsely punctate medially. ->
   `Anthidium pallidiclypeum` (watchlist)

4'. Clypeus with distinct acute sublateral teeth on the distal margin;
    depressed marginal zone of `T5` densely punctate. -> 5

5. Fore and mid basitarsi densely covered with tomentum, obscuring the
   integument; `T6` with a distinct blunt lateral tooth or angle; depressed
   apical rim of `T6` visible in dorsal view; terga shiny. ->
   `Anthidium placitum` (watchlist)

5'. Fore and mid basitarsi not densely tomentose; `T6` without a distinct
    lateral angle; depressed apical rim of `T6` not clearly visible in dorsal
    view; terga duller, with densely punctate maculations. ->
    `Anthidium edwardsii`

6. Fore and mid basitarsi densely tomentose at least basally, usually obscuring
   the integument or making the segments appear robust. -> 7

6'. Fore and mid basitarsi not densely tomentose; integument at least partly
    visible. -> 13

7. `T6` with a broad submedian lobe on the distal margin; tibiae usually light
   reddish brown. -> `Anthidium palmarum`

7'. `T6` without a broad submedian lobe on the distal margin; tibiae yellow,
    black, or black with yellow markings. -> 8

8. Fore basitarsus with a conspicuous fringe of long hairs along the posterior
   margin, about 1.5-2 times the basitarsal width; species associated with sand
   dunes. -> `Anthidium palliventre`

8'. Fore basitarsus without that long posterior fringe, or fringe short. -> 9

9. `T6` distinctly convex in profile, with depressed apical rim completely
   visible across a truncate distal margin; depressed marginal zone of `T3`
   finely and sparsely punctate medially. -> `Anthidium dammersi` (watchlist)

9'. `T6` not with the above combination. -> 10

10. Clypeus prominently convex; `T6` with a distinct median emargination, and
    the depressed apical rim progressively disappearing about halfway between
    the median emargination and the lateral angle. ->
    `Anthidium atrifrons` (watchlist)

10'. Clypeus weakly convex to nearly flat, or `T6` median emargination weak or
     absent, or `T6` apical rim more broadly projected. -> 11

11. Clypeus weakly convex to nearly flat; face and mandible often mostly black;
    `T6` with median emargination absent or only weakly sinuate, and depressed
    apical rim extending across nearly the entire distal margin; terga shiny,
    with a narrow smooth apical border. -> `Anthidium tenuiflorae`

11'. Clypeus more prominently convex or wavy; terga duller or more coarsely
     punctate; `T6` with depressed apical rim shorter or differently projected.
     -> 12

12. Clypeal distal margin wavy, sublateral tubercles not strongly projected;
    mandible except teeth and inferior paraocular area yellow; `T6` depressed
    apical rim distinct along about three-quarters of the distal margin. ->
    `Anthidium collectum`

12'. Clypeus weakly convex with distal margin weakly concave to nearly
     straight, lateral tubercles not strongly projected; `T6` depressed apical
     rim projecting on slightly more than the median half of the distal margin;
     clypeus and mandible usually yellow. ->
     `Anthidium pallidiclypeum` (watchlist; alternate route when the clypeal
     emargination is weak)

13. `T6` narrowly projected at the midapical margin; posterior surface of the
    antennal scape densely covered with short white tomentum; clypeal distal
    margin strongly tuberculate or toothed. ->
    `Anthidium clypeodentatum` (watchlist)

13'. `T6` not narrowly projected at the midapical margin; posterior surface of
     the scape not densely white-tomentose. -> 14

14. Propodeal triangle dull, finely imbricate to lineolate; usually larger
    specimens, often around 11 mm or more; `T6` depressed apical rim projected
    on about the median half of the distal margin. -> `Anthidium mormonum`

14'. Propodeal triangle shiny or nearly smooth; usually smaller specimens. -> 15

15. Clypeus and inferior paraocular area entirely black; `T6` distal margin
    broadly rounded; metasoma sometimes light reddish brown in contrast with
    darker head and mesosoma. -> `Anthidium jocosum` (watchlist)

15'. Clypeus and inferior paraocular area usually yellow-marked; `T6` distal
     margin more truncate, with a conspicuous depressed apical rim. ->
     `Anthidium utahense`

## Key to males

1. Penis valve greatly elongate, extending anteriorly over `S6` and visible
   without dissection; hind coxa flattened ventrally and densely covered with
   short, stout, simple golden to brown hairs; median spine of `T7` short and
   blunt. -> `Anthidium illustre`

1'. Penis valve not greatly elongate and not visible as above. -> 2

2. Hind tibia with a distinct anterior longitudinal carina; frons dull and
   sparsely punctate; `F1` long and slender; `T7` lateral lobe digitiform; `S4`
   without an apical brush. -> `Anthidium maculosum`

2'. Hind tibia without a distinct carina, or carina weak/ambiguous and not
    accompanied by the `A. maculosum` combination above. -> 3

3. `S4` with a broad brush of stout black hairs on the distal margin, at least
   about one-third the width of the sternum. -> 4

3'. `S4` without a broad black brush; brush absent, small, reddish brown, or no
    more than about one-quarter the width of the sternum. -> 9

4. `S8` pointed or only weakly bifid apically, not deeply bifid. -> 5

4'. `S8` deeply bifid apically. -> 7

5. `S6` lateral lobe low to nearly absent; `S4` with dense, long brush occupying
   nearly one-half the width of the segment; `T7` lateral lobe usually apically
   curved; `S8` weakly bifid and gently curved in profile. ->
   `Anthidium palmarum`

5'. `S6` with a distinct acute or subtruncate lateral lobe. -> 6

6. `T7` lateral lobe about as wide as or slightly narrower than the distance
   between its inner margin and the median spine; `S4` brush shorter and
   sparser on a gently concave distal margin; `S6` with subtruncate lateral
   lobe and acute inner angle; `S8` tapering distally and bent strongly in
   profile. -> `Anthidium collectum`

7. Emargination of `T7` between lateral lobe and median spine narrow,
   semicircular or nearly so; `S4` with long black brush; `S6` lateral lobe
   narrow to digitiform; sterna usually with whitish hairs. ->
   `Anthidium tenuiflorae`

7'. Emargination of `T7` broader, not semicircular; `S4` brush made of shorter
    black hairs. -> 8

8. `T7` lateral lobe narrow, basally about as wide as or slightly wider than
   the distance to the median spine; distal margin of `S7` rounded or truncate;
   terga usually shinier, with depressed marginal areas more densely punctate.
   -> `Anthidium atrifrons` (watchlist)

8'. `T7` lateral lobe broader basally, about 1.5 times the distance to the
    median spine, and somewhat tapering distally; distal margin of `S7`
    distinctly pointed; terga duller, with depressed marginal areas more
    sparsely punctate medially. -> `Anthidium dammersi` (watchlist)

9. `S8` lacking a distinct long apical process, distal margin forming only a
   broad median projection; `S6` with small triangular median lobe and no
   lateral lobe; tergal discs finely and sparsely punctate. ->
   `Anthidium clypeodentatum` (watchlist)

9'. `S8` with a distinct apical process, usually bifid or stalked. -> 10

10. `S8` with basally broad bifid apical process, not attached to the disc by a
    long narrow stalk; `S6` median lobe bispinose; `T7` lateral lobe usually
    strongly curved. -> `Anthidium palliventre`

10'. `S8` with a long, narrow stalked apical process, or otherwise not as
     above. -> 11

11. Arms of the bifid apical process of `S8` enlarged or capitate; `S6` lateral
    lobe reduced to nearly absent. -> `Anthidium pallidiclypeum` (watchlist)

11'. Arms of the bifid apical process of `S8` not enlarged or capitate; `S6`
     with a distinct lateral lobe. -> 12

12. `T7` lateral lobe tapering apically, usually narrower than the distance
    between its inner margin and the median spine; outer margin straight and
    inner margin sometimes angled; `S6` lateral lobe short and laterally
    directed, with the margin between lateral and median lobes straight rather
    than semicircular. -> `Anthidium edwardsii`

12'. `T7` lateral lobe rounded or subquadrate, not tapering apically, at least
     as wide as the distance to the median spine; `S6` lateral lobe posteriorly
     or medially directed, with a deeply emarginate or semicircular interval
     between lateral and median lobes. -> 13

13. `T7` lateral lobe subquadrate, about twice as broad as the distance to the
    median spine; `S4` with a narrow reddish brush, about one-sixth sternal
    width; `T7` distinctly and contiguously punctate, appearing rugulose. -> 14

13'. `T7` lateral lobe subtriangular or elongate with rounded to slightly
     curved apex, about 1.5 times the distance to the median spine; `S4` with a
     broader reddish brush, about one-quarter sternal width; `T7` shallower
     punctate and smoother. -> 15

14. `S4` brush dense and easy to see among the surrounding sternal hairs; `S6`
    lateral lobe acute, usually darker and more strongly sclerotized than the
    median lobe. -> `Anthidium utahense`

14'. `S4` brush sparse and difficult to see among surrounding sternal hairs;
     `S6` lateral lobe blunt and broader, often not distinctly darker or more
     sclerotized than the median lobe. -> `Anthidium jocosum` (watchlist)

15. Median lobe of `S6` narrowly rounded or subtruncate, often apically notched;
    lateral lobe of `S6` curved and medially directed in ventral view; arms of
    the bifid apical process of `S8` gently curved in posterior view; inner
    orbits of eyes parallel. -> `Anthidium placitum` (watchlist)

15'. Median lobe of `S6` broadly rounded and entire; lateral lobe of `S6`
     straight and posteriorly directed in ventral view; arms of the bifid
     apical process of `S8` diverging in posterior view; inner orbits of eyes
     diverging dorsally. -> `Anthidium mormonum`

## Notes

- `Anthidium pallidiclypeum` appears twice in the female key because Gonzalez
  and Griswold (2013) note variation in the clypeal emargination.
- Male `A. edwardsii` may show a weak hind tibial carina. If a specimen with an
  ambiguous carina does not match `A. maculosum`, continue through the
  no-carina path and compare `A. edwardsii`.
- For `A. mormonum`, `A. utahense`, and `A. jocosum`, male sternites and `T7`
  are especially important. Female color alone is not enough.
- `A. palliventre` is the strongest coastal-dune candidate in this regional
  set; its female fore-basitarsal fringe is a particularly useful character.

## Document Provenance and AI Assistance Statement

Prepared on 2026-07-31 as an AI-assisted working key for the
`ai-insect-keys` repository. Codex was used to inspect the repository, extract
and summarize characters from the cited sources, draft the reduced regional
couplets, add IDtools confirmation checks, and organize the Markdown file.

The primary taxonomic authority for the couplets is Gonzalez and Griswold
(2013), supplemented by the `Exotic Bee ID` fact sheets and key listed below.
