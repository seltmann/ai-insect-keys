# California Bembicidae Genus Key

Last updated: 2026-08-12

## Scope

This is the family-level home for California Bembicidae in this repository. It
pulls the Bembicidae genus-key section out of the broader spheciform-wasp key
so this folder can treat the whole family, not only `Bembix`.

Included California genera: `Alysson`, `Arigorytes`, `Bembecinus`, `Bembix`,
`Bicyrtes`, `Didineis`, `Epinysson`, `Foxia`, `Glenostictia`, `Gorytes`,
`Hapalomellinus`, `Harpactus`, `Hoplisoides`, `Hyponysson`, `Metanysson`,
`Microbembex`, `Nysson`, `Oryttus`, `Sphecius`, `Steniolia`, `Stictiella`,
`Stizoides`, `Stizus`, `Trichogorytes`, `Xerostictia`, and `Zanysson`.

This is a working key. Confirm difficult endpoints against the cited sources,
especially for small gorytines, nyssonines, and any specimen with damaged wing
venation or obscured sternal characters.

## Field-Photo Genus Characters To Capture

The Southern Forest Life crabronid photo guide is Australia-focused, so this
section uses it only as a practical field-photo checklist. Do not treat its
examples as California distribution records or species endpoints.

| Character area | What to capture | Why it matters here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Face and eye margins | Straight-on face view showing whether the inner eye margins are parallel, converging, or notched; include antennal sockets and clypeus. | Helps with gorytine, stizine, and bembicine routes, especially when the specimen looks bee-like in habitus. |
| Ocelli | Dorsal or oblique head view showing normal lenses, reduced scars, slitlike remnants, or ocelli in a depressed field. | Important for Bembicini, Stictiellina, `Bembix`, and several species-level `Bembix` decisions. |
| Labrum | Front or side view showing whether the labrum is exserted, elongate, hidden, dentate, or smooth. | Exserted or elongate labrum helps place `Bembix`; labrum profile separates several `Bembix` species. |
| Wing venation | Flat forewing view showing submarginal cell count and shape, whether cell II is petiolate, reduced, or triangular, recurrent-vein placement, and M relative to cu-a. | Used repeatedly across Alyssonini, Nyssonini, Gorytini, Bembicina, and species-level `Bembix`. |
| Propodeum and metasoma | Lateral or dorsolateral view of propodeal margins and the first metasomal segment. | Helps separate `Bembecinus`, gorytines, `Bicyrtes`, `Microbembex`, and pedunculate forms. |
| Sex-linked structures | Antennal segment count, male sterna and terminalia when visible, and female fore-basitarsal rake. | Prevents mixing male and female key routes; female rake counts are central in `Bembix`. |

## Key To California Tribes Of Bembicidae

This is the California-pruned form of the historical Nyssoninae tribal key;
Mellinini is treated separately here as Mellinidae.

| Couplet | Character state | Go to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1a | Sternum I with two ridges diverging posteriorly from between the hind coxae, or one ridge that forks near midlength; submarginal cell II petiolate or forewing with only two submarginal cells; admedian lines nearly fused into one median groove. | 2 |
| 1b | Sternum I basomedially simple or with a single ridge that does not fork posteriorly. | 3 |
| 2a | Oblique scutal carina present; body sculpture usually coarse; pronotal collar ridgelike. | Nyssonini |
| 2b | Oblique scutal carina absent; body sculpture generally finer except on propodeum; pronotal collar broadly rounded. | Alyssonini |
| 3a | Labrum exserted and at least as long as broad; omaulus absent; hindocelli deformed or scar-like; only one midtibial spur; prestigmal portion of submarginal cell I more than twice the cell height. | Bembicini |
| 3b | Labrum, ocelli, and midtibial spur combination not as above. | 4 |
| 4a | Prestigmal portion of submarginal cell I more than twice the cell height; omaulus absent; propodeal enclosure extends far onto the vertical slope; scutellum overlaps metanotum with a lamelliform edge. | Stizini |
| 4b | Without the complete stizine combination above; oblique scutal carina commonly present. | Gorytini |

## Alyssonini - 2 Genera

Genera: `Alysson`, `Didineis`

| Couplet | Character state | Go to / genus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1a | Forewing M diverges at or beyond cu-a; male terminal flagellomere incurved but not opposed by a strong projection from flagellomere XII; metapleuron about half as long as high; tergum II almost always with paired pale spots. | `Alysson` |
| 1b | Forewing M diverges before cu-a by at least the length of cu-a; male terminal flagellomere strongly incurved and opposed by a projection from XII; metapleuron much less than half as long as high; tergum II without pale spots. | `Didineis` |

Primary basis: Bohart and Menke (1976), Alyssonini, pp. 453-460.

## Nyssonini - 6 Genera

Genera: `Epinysson`, `Foxia`, `Hyponysson`, `Metanysson`, `Nysson`, `Zanysson`

Current `Epinysson` concepts should be checked against the modern catalog; some
species were separated under `Synnevrus` in Bohart and Menke.

| Couplet | Character state | Go to / genus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1a | Posterior surface of hind tibia with teeth or stout spines. | 2 |
| 1b | Posterior surface of hind tibia with hairs and bristles only. | 4 |
| 2a | Forewing with two submarginal cells; female sternum VI reduced to a linear ridge; tergal margins edged with close-set flattened setae. | `Metanysson` |
| 2b | Forewing with three submarginal cells. | 3 |
| 3a | Hind tibia with a long row of stout teeth; inner orbit rather evenly concave; tergal margins thickened but without a fringe of flattened setae. | `Zanysson` |
| 3b | Hind tibia with only a few stout teeth. This state does not lead to another genus in the working California list; recheck `Metanysson` or `Zanysson`, or use the full key. | Formal key / recheck |
| 4a | Sterna II-IV laterally lobate, dentate, or spinose; second recurrent vein ends well beyond submarginal cell II. | `Foxia` |
| 4b | Sterna II-IV not laterally lobate, dentate, or spinose. | 5 |
| 5a | Forewing with two submarginal cells, the second petiolate. | `Hyponysson` |
| 5b | Forewing with three submarginal cells, the second petiolate. | 6 |
| 6a | Posterior tergal margins simple, without strong double edging; second recurrent vein ends at or only slightly beyond submarginal cell II; female pygidial plate well developed. | `Nysson` |
| 6b | Posterior tergal margins thickened and double-edged dorsolaterally; hind-wing M usually diverges beyond cu-a. | `Epinysson` [confirm] |

Primary basis: Bohart and Menke (1976), New World Nyssonini, pp. 461-480.

## Gorytini - 8 Genera

Genera: `Arigorytes`, `Gorytes`, `Hapalomellinus`, `Harpactus`, `Hoplisoides`,
`Oryttus`, `Sphecius`, `Trichogorytes`

`Arigorytes` and some small gorytines are subtle; confirm equivocal endpoints
with the Nearctic revisions.

| Couplet | Character state | Go to / genus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1a | Very large wasp, commonly 20-40 mm; broad yellow-banded metasoma and amber wings; cicada hunter. | `Sphecius` |
| 1b | Smaller; not a cicada-killer form. | 2 |
| 2a | Hind-wing M diverges at or very near cu-a; male sternum VIII bispinose. | `Oryttus` |
| 2b | Hind-wing M diverges distinctly beyond cu-a. | 3 |
| 3a | Pleuron and much of body densely covered with silvery appressed pubescence that obscures sculpture; marginal cell blunt or rounded. | `Trichogorytes` |
| 3b | Body not densely obscured by silvery appressed pubescence. | 4 |
| 4a | Inner eye margins strongly converge below; metasoma distinctly pedunculate; oblique scutal carina present. | `Hapalomellinus` |
| 4b | Specimen lacking that complete combination. | 5 |
| 5a | Propodeal enclosure strongly sculptured, with longitudinal ridges extending along most of the anterior sulcus. | `Gorytes` |
| 5b | Propodeal enclosure not with that ridge pattern. | 6 |
| 6a | Acetabular carina present but subomaulus not continued ventrally; males commonly with concealed hairbrushes on sterna V-VI. | `Hoplisoides` |
| 6b | Mesopleural and male sternal combination not as above. | 7 |
| 7a | Marginal cell acute; integument not densely silvery; genus historically keyed as `Dienoplus` in Bohart and Menke. | `Harpactus` |
| 7b | Stocky western Nearctic gorytine; oblique scutal carina absent and frons relatively narrow near the midocellus. | `Arigorytes` [confirm] |

Primary basis: Bohart and Menke (1976), Gorytini, pp. 486-522; Pate (1947) and
Bohart (1971) for `Arigorytes`.

## Stizini - 3 Genera

Genera: `Bembecinus`, `Stizoides`, `Stizus`

| Couplet | Character state | Go to / genus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1a | Propodeum compressed into a posterolateral ridge, usually notched; episternal-scrobal sulcus absent; hind-wing M diverges close to cu-a; male flagellomere IX commonly with a spinelike projection. | `Bembecinus` |
| 1b | Propodeum not compressed into a posterolateral ridge; episternal-scrobal sulcus present; hind-wing M diverges far before cu-a; male flagellomere IX without a spinelike projection. | 2 |
| 2a | Inner eye margins only slightly closer below than above; mandible subapically dentate; low double convexity below the midocellus. | `Stizus` |
| 2b | Inner eye margins strongly converge below; mandible simple; prominent frontal hump below the midocellus. | `Stizoides` |

Primary basis: Bohart and Menke (1976), Stizini, pp. 523-531.

## Bembicini: First Determine The Subtribe

| Couplet | Character state | Go to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1a | Midocellus or its remnant lies in a conspicuous pit or depressed field. | Stictiellina |
| 1b | Midocellus not situated in a conspicuous pit; reduced ocelli or scars lie approximately level with the surrounding integument. | Bembicina |

## Bembicina - 3 Genera

Genera: `Bembix`, `Bicyrtes`, `Microbembex`

| Couplet | Character state | Go to / genus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1a | Distal half of marginal cell bends away from the wing margin; propodeal lateral angles do not project; palpi reduced. | `Microbembex` |
| 1b | Marginal cell follows the wing margin. | 2 |
| 2a | Propodeal lateral angles project; robust, short-faced sand wasp; palpal formula 6-4. | `Bicyrtes` |
| 2b | Propodeum flatter; midocellar area a transverse scar or crescent-shaped lens; labrum very long and beaklike. | `Bembix` |

## Stictiellina - 4 Genera

Genera: `Glenostictia`, `Steniolia`, `Stictiella`, `Xerostictia`

| Couplet | Character state | Go to / genus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1a | Palpi reduced: maxillary palpus with four or fewer segments and labial palpus with two or fewer. | 2 |
| 1b | Palpal formula 6-4. | 3 |
| 2a | Labrum evenly convex and about twice its basal breadth; maxillary palpus four-segmented. | `Xerostictia` |
| 2b | Labrum swollen basally; maxillary palpus three-segmented. | `Steniolia` |
| 3a | Midocellar plane broader than long; female clypeus strongly convex; male midfemur serrate or emarginate. | `Stictiella` |
| 3b | Midocellar plane not broader than long; female clypeus low or beveled; male midfemur rounded. | `Glenostictia` |

Primary basis: Bohart and Horning (1971), California bembicine sand wasps;
Bohart and Gillaspy (1985), California Stictiellina; Bohart and Menke (1976),
pp. 532-554.
