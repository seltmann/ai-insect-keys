# Eastern Sierra Chrysididae Genus Key

Last updated: 2026-08-25

## Scope

This is a working key to the Chrysididae genera represented in the
Kimsey-based Eastern Sierra checklist:
`checklists/chrysididae/eastern-sierra-working-list.md`.

The key follows Kimsey (2006) and is filtered to the current Eastern Sierra
working fauna. Because the Kimsey checklist records all California chrysidid
genera from Inyo or Mono County, this is also effectively a California genus
key for the genera treated in that monograph.

Use this as a specimen key, not as a field-photo shortcut. Several couplets
require the ventral surface, tarsal claws, wing venation, mouthparts, scapal
basin sculpture, or tergum III.

## Integrated Genus Key

1a. Metasoma with five visible segments in males and four in females; sternum
strongly convex; face above antennal sockets convex, without a scapal basin;
pronotum narrowed submedially and crossed by a transverse groove.
`Cleptes`

1b. Metasoma with four or fewer visible segments in males and three in females;
sternum flat or concave; face above antennal sockets flat or concave, usually
with a scapal basin; pronotum not narrowed submedially.
Go to 2.

2a. Tongue elongate and tubular, as long as the head or longer; male metasoma
with four external segments and female metasoma with three; apical tergum
broadly V-shaped and denticulate, with two large lateral subapical foveae.
`Parnopes`

2b. Tongue not elongate or tubular, fitting closely in the oral fossa; metasoma
with three external segments in both sexes; apical tergum rounded, notched, or
with one to six teeth or lobes, and lacking the two large lateral subapical
foveae of `Parnopes`.
Go to 3.

3a. Tergum III with a subapical pit row, sometimes weak; mesopleuron with a
horizontal scrobal sulcus; forewing Rs stub sclerotized for more than half the
length of the marginal cell, with discoidal and cubital cells usually complete;
tarsal claws edentate.
Go to the Chrysidini route.

3b. Tergum III without a subapical pit row; mesopleuron with an oblique scrobal
sulcus; forewing Rs stub sclerotized for less than half the length of the
marginal cell, without a discoidal cell; tarsal claws usually toothed, except
some distinctive reduced or red-bodied forms.
Go to the Elampini route.

## Chrysidini Route

C1a. Tergum III apical rim with four or six teeth or angles, or with four weak
lobes.
`Chrysis`

C1b. Tergum III apical rim with zero to three teeth or angles.
Go to C2.

C2a. Tergum III apical rim with a medial notch and a lateral angle just beyond the
pit row; transverse frontal carina absent or only slightly indicated.
`Chrysurissa`

C2b. Tergum III apical rim, if notched, without a lateral angle just beyond the
pit row; transverse frontal carina various.
Go to C3.

C3a. Tergum III apical rim edentate, or rarely with a weak lateral angle; face
unusually flattened and completely granulate-punctate, without fine median
cross-ridging; transverse frontal carina absent.
`Chrysura`

C3b. Tergum III apical rim dentate or medially notched, or if nearly edentate
then face with median cross-ridging and/or a transverse frontal carina.
Go to C4.

C4a. Scapal basin with a pit in a sublateral depression above the antennal
socket, sometimes weakly indicated in small species with an incomplete
forewing discoidal cell; tergum III apical rim often tridentate.
`Caenochrysis`

C4b. Scapal basin without an unusual sublateral depression or pit; forewing
discoidal cell complete; tergum III apical rim various but not tridentate.
Go to C5.

C5a. Tibiae and often tegula and/or tergum III partly whitish, or metanotum
sharply angulate or somewhat projecting backward in profile; vertex without
four smooth warts or swellings.
`Argochrysis`

C5b. Tibiae, tegula, and tergum III not partly whitish; metanotum rounded in
lateral view; vertex sometimes with four smooth warts or swellings.
`Ceratochrysis`

## Elampini Route

E1a. Hindtarsal claw edentate and metasoma non-metallic red.
`Xerochrum`

E1b. Hindtarsal claw with one to five subsidiary teeth; metasoma metallic green,
blue, purple, or non-metallic black.
Go to E2.

E2a. Forewing Rs vein reduced to a short stub, less than one-fifth the length of
the medial vein.
Go to E3.

E3a. Forewing venation restricted to the basal third of the wing; pronotum
without lateral carina; face evenly roughened and somewhat striate, without a
discrete scapal basin; body tiny and non-metallic black.
`Microchridium`

E3b. Forewing venation extending half or more of the wing length; pronotum with
lateral carina; face smooth with scattered punctures; scapal basin clearly
indicated, polished, and impunctate.
`Minymischa`

E2b. Forewing Rs vein one-half or more as long as the medial vein.
Go to E4.

E4a. Hindtarsal claw with one perpendicular, submedial tooth; face flat or
slightly concave medially, with a narrow to broad zone of fine, dense
cross-ridging in the scapal basin.
`Hedychridium`

E4b. Hindtarsal claw with a single subparallel subsidiary tooth or with more than
one subsidiary tooth; face usually strongly concave medially; scapal basin
smooth and polished, finely punctate, or marked by coarse cross-ridging.
Go to E5.

E5a. Scapal basin with dense tiny punctures covered with dense appressed silvery
pubescence; tarsal claw with one subparallel tooth; forewing medial vein
arising at cu-a.
`Hedychreides`

E5b. Scapal basin either smooth and impunctate or with at least a narrow zone of
cross-ridging, without dense appressed silvery pubescence; tarsal claw
dentition various; forewing medial vein usually arising before or after cu-a.
Go to E6.

E6a. Forewing medial vein straight or weakly curved medially; hindtarsal claw
with one subsidiary tooth, except in some males.
Go to E7.

E7a. Head with a sharp tubercle at the base of the oral fossa; male hindfemur
enlarged and non-metallic black or brown; female sternum III with transverse
subbasal sulci and often an apicomedial lobe or tubercle; mid and hind tibiae
usually with a discrete pit on the inner surface.
`Hedychrum`

E7b. Head without a tubercle at the base of the oral fossa; male hindfemur not
enlarged or non-metallic; female sternum III unmodified; mid and hind tibiae
without a pit on the inner surface.
`Pseudolopyga`

E6b. Forewing medial vein strongly arched, often forming a right angle;
hindtarsal claw usually with two or more subsidiary teeth.
Go to E8.

E8a. Forewing medial cell setose; mesopleuron strongly angulate, with scrobal
carina, verticaulus, and omaulus meeting in a ventrally pointing angle.
`Holopyga`

E8b. Forewing medial cell asetose; mesopleuron usually not strongly angulate;
scrobal carina, verticaulus, and omaulus not meeting in a ventrally pointing
angle.
Go to E9.

E9a. Metanotum medially mucronate and flat dorsally; scutum coarsely and
irregularly punctate; tergum III produced apically into a snout-like process;
scapal basin flat or shallowly concave, with U-shaped cross-ridges or wrinkles.
`Elampus`

E9b. Metanotum rounded or conical; tergum III usually without a truncate snout;
scutum with sparse punctures clumped along or between notauli, or entirely
absent; scapal basin usually deeply concave and smooth.
Go to E10.

E10a. Scutum medially impunctate or with small, evenly scattered punctures;
mesopleuron with scrobal sulcus foreshortened; omaulus elongate and extending
nearly horizontally to the scrobal sulcus; tergum III usually with a transparent
rim and usually without an apicomedial notch.
`Omalus`

E10b. Scutum with large punctures, especially between or along the notauli;
mesopleuron with scrobal sulcus and omaulus subequal in length and extending
at an oblique angle ventrally; tergum III usually without a transparent rim and
often with an apicomedial notch.
Go to E11.

E11a. Scutum with large punctures clumped along the notauli; malar space not
divided by the genal carina.
`Philoctetes`

E11b. Scutum with large, shallow punctures clumped posteriorly between the
notauli; malar space divided by the genal carina.
`Pseudomalus`

## Practical Checkpoints

| First character to check | Most useful split |
| --- | --- |
| Visible metasomal segment count | `Cleptes` versus Chrysidinae |
| Long tubular tongue | `Parnopes` |
| Tergum III subapical pit row | Chrysidini versus Elampini |
| Tergum III with four or six teeth | `Chrysis` |
| Red non-metallic metasoma plus edentate hindtarsal claw | `Xerochrum` |
| Highly reduced forewing venation and tiny black body | `Microchridium` |
| Snout-like tergum III and mucronate metanotum | `Elampus` |
