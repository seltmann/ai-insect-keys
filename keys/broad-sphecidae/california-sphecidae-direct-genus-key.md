# Direct Key to the California Genera of Sphecidae

Last updated: 2026-08-10

Scope. This is a direct genus key for California specimens already determined
as Sphecidae in the restricted modern sense. It covers the 10 California genera
treated in the broad-Sphecidae working pages:

`Ammophila`, `Chalybion`, `Chlorion`, `Isodontia`, `Palmodes`, `Podalonia`,
`Podium`, `Prionyx`, `Sceliphron`, and `Sphex`.

This version avoids starting from higher-level family or tribe couplets. It
assumes the specimen is already sorted to restricted Sphecidae and uses direct
generic characters from Bohart and Menke's Sphecinae genus keys and generic
descriptions.

| 2a | Both recurrent veins received by the second submarginal cell; spiracular groove absent; body usually with yellow areas; female without a foretarsal rake. | `Sceliphron` |
| 2b | Second recurrent vein received by the third submarginal cell, or interstitial between the second and third; spiracular groove present; body without yellow, often metallic blue or green; female with a foretarsal rake. | 3 |

| 5b | Hind-wing third anal vein and episternal sulcus not matching `Podium`. | 6 |

| 6b | Apex of sternum I not meeting and overlapping the base of sternum II, or the specimen otherwise lacks the `Podalonia` combination. | 7 |
| 7a | Apex of sternum I does not reach the base of sternum II; intervening space usually long and membranous with a ligament; spiracle of tergum I at or beyond the level of the sternum-I apex; sternum I usually straight or bent downward at the base of tergum I. | `Ammophila` |
| 7b | Sternum-I and tergum-I arrangement not matching `Ammophila` or `Podalonia`. | 8 |

| 8b | Propodeal side without a complete spiracular groove, or with a groove incomplete and not extending more than halfway from hind coxa to spiracle. | 9 |

| 9b | Basal veinlet of forewing submarginal cell II not longer than the anterior veinlet, or the specimen lacks the `Prionyx` combination. | 10 |

| 10b | Claw teeth obliquely oriented to the inner margin; petiole equal to or longer than hindtarsomeres II-IV; anterior veinlet of forewing submarginal cell III usually conspicuously longer than the posterobasal veinlet. | `Isodontia` |

## Fast Genus Checks

| Genus | Practical confirmation notes |
| --- | --- |
| `Ammophila` | Sternum I does not reach sternum II; tergum-I spiracle at or beyond the sternum-I apex. |
| `Podalonia` | Sternum I reaches or overlaps sternum II; tergum-I spiracle before the sternum-I apex. |
| `Sceliphron` | U-shaped propodeal enclosure, both recurrent veins received by submarginal cell II, no spiracular groove. |
| `Chalybion` | Hind-wing third anal vein long and separated from the margin; episternal sulcus long. |
| `Podium` | Hind-wing third anal vein short and contiguous with the margin; episternal sulcus short. |
| `Chlorion` | U-shaped propodeal enclosure plus second recurrent vein received by cell III or interstitial; female clypeus usually five-toothed. |
| `Prionyx` | Propodeal spiracular groove absent; basal veinlet of submarginal cell II longer than anterior veinlet; inner eye margins straight or broadly bowed inward; inner hind-tibial spur usually coarse and widely spaced; hindtarsal claw with two to five inner teeth; male placoids present in about half of species. |
| `Palmodes` | Female clypeal median lobe truncate or shallowly concave with lateral notches; males lack flagellar placoids. |
| `Sphex` | Complete propodeal spiracular groove; petiole shorter than hindtarsomeres II-IV combined; basal veinlet of submarginal cell II equal to or shorter than anterior veinlet; inner eye margins straight below and curving inward near ocelli; inner hind-tibial spur usually finely and closely pectinate; male antenna usually with one or more placoids. |
| `Isodontia` | Incomplete or absent propodeal spiracular groove, longer petiole, and oblique claw teeth. |

## Notes For Difficult Specimens

- This direct key does not repeat the Sphecinae tribe key. If a specimen fails
  several couplets, return to the full Bohart and Menke keys before forcing an
  endpoint.
- The `Podium` / `Chlorion` separation should be confirmed structurally. Color
  helps, but the hind-wing third anal vein, episternal sulcus, clypeal teeth,
  and antennal socket position are more important.
- For males, antennal characters may matter at later species-level steps even
  when the genus is clear.
- Male placoids are important in `Sphex`, but they are not exclusive to
  `Sphex`. Bohart and Menke also describe male placoids in `Isodontia` and in
  about half of `Prionyx`; use them with the propodeal groove, petiole, venation,
  and claw characters.
- The final `Palmodes` / `Isodontia` path includes sex-specific characters.
  When only a male is available, flagellar placoids and venation become
  especially important.
