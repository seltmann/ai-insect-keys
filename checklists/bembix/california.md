# Checklist: California Bembix species and Santa Barbara County relevance

Last updated: 2026-07-31

## Scope

This is a working checklist of California species of `Bembix` for the
insect-identification keys in this repository, with emphasis on taxa relevant
to a female specimen from coastal Santa Barbara County or the Dangermond
Preserve that has conspicuously spiny forelegs.

The checklist treats species as the primary units and lists named California
forms of `Bembix americana` separately because those subspecies are important
for Santa Barbara County and Channel Islands identifications.

## Current interpretation

Bohart and Horning (1971) treated 11 California species of `Bembix`. A search
for species described or added after that revision found one additional valid
California species, `Bembix inyoensis` Kimsey and Kimsey, 1981. The same search
found one later-described California subspecies, `Bembix americana dugi` Menke
in Rust, Menke, and Miller, 1985, from San Clemente Island.

The resulting working checklist is 12 California `Bembix` species, plus named
California `B. americana` subspecies. No other post-1971 California `Bembix`
species were found in Pulawski's current catalog or in targeted literature
searches on 2026-07-31. `Bembix boharti` Griswold, 1983 was checked carefully
because it is a nearby post-1971 species, but its type series and discussion
place it in Baja California, not California.

For a mainland coastal Santa Barbara County or Dangermond Preserve female with
conspicuously spiny forelegs, the strongest checklist candidate is
`Bembix americana comata`. If the specimen is from the northern Channel Islands
rather than the mainland, `Bembix americana hamata` becomes important.

## Taxa checked but not added as California species

| Taxon | Result | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Bembix boharti` Griswold, 1983 | Excluded from the California checklist for now. | Described from Lower California; type material is from Baja California Norte and Baja California Sur. Griswold states that it appears restricted to central and southern Lower California and is isolated from `B. sayi` by intervening desert areas. |
| `Bembix americana spinolae` Lepeletier, 1845 | Not counted as a separate California species; retained as a taxonomic note under `B. americana`. | Bohart and Horning discuss eastern and northern California populations of `B. a. comata` as showing tendencies toward `spinolae`, but do not treat it as a separate California checklist entry. |
| `Bembix cameroni` J. Parker, 1917 | Not added. | Bohart and Horning considered California reports unverified and removed it from the California `Bembix` key. |

## Notes on newly described and post-1971 taxa

- `Bembix inyoensis` is the only post-1971 species found for California in the
  current search. The original description was confirmed bibliographically, but
  a full accessible copy was not available during this pass. The checklist
  therefore cites Pulawski for type locality and taxonomic status and treats
  female foreleg characters as not scored until the original description is
  reviewed.
- `Bembix americana dugi` is a post-1971 subspecies, not an added species. It
  matters for a complete California checklist because it is a named California
  Channel Islands form from San Clemente Island.
- `Bembix boharti` has seven female fore-basitarsal rake setae and is close to
  `B. sayi`, but current literature places it outside California.
