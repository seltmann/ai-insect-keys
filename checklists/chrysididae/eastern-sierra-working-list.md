# Eastern Sierra Chrysididae Working List

Last updated: 2026-08-25

## Scope

This checklist is a source-grounded working list of Chrysididae recorded from the eastern Sierra and adjacent east-side California habitats. For this pass, the operational boundary is any `Inyo Co.` or `Mono Co.` record in Kimsey (2006), including Owens Valley, Deep Springs, the White Mountains, Mono Basin, east-side Sierra localities, and desert-margin Inyo localities such as Death Valley, Panamint, Eureka Valley, Saline Valley, and Owens Lake.

This is intentionally a locality-evidence checklist, not a final ecological range treatment. Some printed county/locality combinations in the source look historically broad or potentially mismatched, so questionable combinations should be checked against specimens or original labels before being used as narrow distribution claims.

- Taxa captured from Kimsey (2006): 102.
- With Inyo County evidence: 80.
- With Mono County evidence: 62.

## Review Flags

- Verify apparent county/locality mismatches before downstream use. Examples in the extracted source include `Mammoth Lake` appearing under `Inyo Co.` for some taxa and White Mountains localities appearing under both counties.
- Treat broad Inyo desert records separately from montane east-side Sierra records when building ecological filters or image-identification expectations.
- For specimen identifications, use the Kimsey keys and figures directly; this file is a regional occurrence scaffold, not a diagnostic key.
