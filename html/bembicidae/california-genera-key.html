California Bembicidae Genus Key

Last updated: 2026-08-12

Scope

This is the family-level home for California Bembicidae in this repository. It pulls the Bembicidae genus-key section out of the broader spheciform-wasp key so this folder can treat the whole family, not only Bembix.

Included California genera: Alysson, Arigorytes, Bembecinus, Bembix, Bicyrtes, Didineis, Epinysson, Foxia, Glenostictia, Gorytes, Hapalomellinus, Harpactus, Hoplisoides, Hyponysson, Metanysson, Microbembex, Nysson, Oryttus, Sphecius, Steniolia, Stictiella, Stizoides, Stizus, Trichogorytes, Xerostictia, and Zanysson.

This is a working key. Confirm difficult endpoints against the cited sources, especially for small gorytines, nyssonines, and any specimen with damaged wing venation or obscured sternal characters.

Field-Photo Genus Characters To Capture

The Southern Forest Life crabronid photo guide is Australia-focused, so this section uses it only as a practical field-photo checklist. Do not treat its examples as California distribution records or species endpoints.

Character areaWhat to captureWhy it matters here
Face and eye marginsStraight-on face view showing whether the inner eye margins are parallel, converging, or notched; include antennal sockets and clypeus.Helps with gorytine, stizine, and bembicine routes, especially when the specimen looks bee-like in habitus.
OcelliDorsal or oblique head view showing normal lenses, reduced scars, slitlike remnants, or ocelli in a depressed field.Important for Bembicini, Stictiellina, Bembix, and several species-level Bembix decisions.
LabrumFront or side view showing whether the labrum is exserted, elongate, hidden, dentate, or smooth.Exserted or elongate labrum helps place Bembix; labrum profile separates several Bembix species.
Wing venationFlat forewing view showing submarginal cell count and shape, whether cell II is petiolate, reduced, or triangular, recurrent-vein placement, and M relative to cu-a.Used repeatedly across Alyssonini, Nyssonini, Gorytini, Bembicina, and species-level Bembix.
Propodeum and metasomaLateral or dorsolateral view of propodeal margins and the first metasomal segment.Helps separate Bembecinus, gorytines, Bicyrtes, Microbembex, and pedunculate forms.
Sex-linked structuresAntennal segment count, male sterna and terminalia when visible, and female fore-basitarsal rake.Prevents mixing male and female key routes; female rake counts are central in Bembix.

Key To California Tribes Of Bembicidae

This is the California-pruned form of the historical Nyssoninae tribal key; Mellinini is treated separately here as Mellinidae.

CoupletCharacter stateGo to
1aSternum I with two ridges diverging posteriorly from between the hind coxae, or one ridge that forks near midlength; submarginal cell II petiolate or forewing with only two submarginal cells; admedian lines nearly fused into one median groove.2
1bSternum I basomedially simple or with a single ridge that does not fork posteriorly.3
2aOblique scutal carina present; body sculpture usually coarse; pronotal collar ridgelike.Nyssonini
2bOblique scutal carina absent; body sculpture generally finer except on propodeum; pronotal collar broadly rounded.Alyssonini
3aLabrum exserted and at least as long as broad; omaulus absent; hindocelli deformed or scar-like; only one midtibial spur; prestigmal portion of submarginal cell I more than twice the cell height.Bembicini
3bLabrum, ocelli, and midtibial spur combination not as above.4
4aPrestigmal portion of submarginal cell I more than twice the cell height; omaulus absent; propodeal enclosure extends far onto the vertical slope; scutellum overlaps metanotum with a lamelliform edge.Stizini
4bWithout the complete stizine combination above; oblique scutal carina commonly present.Gorytini

Alyssonini - 2 Genera

Genera: Alysson, Didineis

CoupletCharacter stateGo to / genus
1aForewing M diverges at or beyond cu-a; male terminal flagellomere incurved but not opposed by a strong projection from flagellomere XII; metapleuron about half as long as high; tergum II almost always with paired pale spots.Alysson
1bForewing M diverges before cu-a by at least the length of cu-a; male terminal flagellomere strongly incurved and opposed by a projection from XII; metapleuron much less than half as long as high; tergum II without pale spots.Didineis

Primary basis: Bohart and Menke (1976), Alyssonini, pp. 453-460.

Nyssonini - 6 Genera

Genera: Epinysson, Foxia, Hyponysson, Metanysson, Nysson, Zanysson

Current Epinysson concepts should be checked against the modern catalog; some species were separated under Synnevrus in Bohart and Menke.

CoupletCharacter stateGo to / genus
1aPosterior surface of hind tibia with teeth or stout spines.2
1bPosterior surface of hind tibia with hairs and bristles only.4
2aForewing with two submarginal cells; female sternum VI reduced to a linear ridge; tergal margins edged with close-set flattened setae.Metanysson
2bForewing with three submarginal cells.3

4aSterna II-IV laterally lobate, dentate, or spinose; second recurrent vein ends well beyond submarginal cell II.Foxia
4bSterna II-IV not laterally lobate, dentate, or spinose.5
5aForewing with two submarginal cells, the second petiolate.Hyponysson
5bForewing with three submarginal cells, the second petiolate.6
6aPosterior tergal margins simple, without strong double edging; second recurrent vein ends at or only slightly beyond submarginal cell II; female pygidial plate well developed.Nysson

Gorytini - 8 Genera

Genera: Arigorytes, Gorytes, Hapalomellinus, Harpactus, Hoplisoides, Oryttus, Sphecius, Trichogorytes

Arigorytes and some small gorytines are subtle; confirm equivocal endpoints with the Nearctic revisions.

CoupletCharacter stateGo to / genus
1aVery large wasp, commonly 20-40 mm; broad yellow-banded metasoma and amber wings; cicada hunter.Sphecius
1bSmaller; not a cicada-killer form.2
2aHind-wing M diverges at or very near cu-a; male sternum VIII bispinose.Oryttus
2bHind-wing M diverges distinctly beyond cu-a.3
3aPleuron and much of body densely covered with silvery appressed pubescence that obscures sculpture; marginal cell blunt or rounded.Trichogorytes
3bBody not densely obscured by silvery appressed pubescence.4
4aInner eye margins strongly converge below; metasoma distinctly pedunculate; oblique scutal carina present.Hapalomellinus
4bSpecimen lacking that complete combination.5
5aPropodeal enclosure strongly sculptured, with longitudinal ridges extending along most of the anterior sulcus.Gorytes
5bPropodeal enclosure not with that ridge pattern.6
6aAcetabular carina present but subomaulus not continued ventrally; males commonly with concealed hairbrushes on sterna V-VI.Hoplisoides
6bMesopleural and male sternal combination not as above.7
7aMarginal cell acute; integument not densely silvery; genus historically keyed as Dienoplus in Bohart and Menke.Harpactus
7bStocky western Nearctic gorytine; oblique scutal carina absent and frons relatively narrow near the midocellus.Arigorytes [confirm]

Primary basis: Bohart and Menke (1976), Gorytini, pp. 486-522; Pate (1947) and Bohart (1971) for Arigorytes.

Stizini - 3 Genera

Genera: Bembecinus, Stizoides, Stizus

CoupletCharacter stateGo to / genus
1aPropodeum compressed into a posterolateral ridge, usually notched; episternal-scrobal sulcus absent; hind-wing M diverges close to cu-a; male flagellomere IX commonly with a spinelike projection.Bembecinus
1bPropodeum not compressed into a posterolateral ridge; episternal-scrobal sulcus present; hind-wing M diverges far before cu-a; male flagellomere IX without a spinelike projection.2
2aInner eye margins only slightly closer below than above; mandible subapically dentate; low double convexity below the midocellus.Stizus
2bInner eye margins strongly converge below; mandible simple; prominent frontal hump below the midocellus.Stizoides

Primary basis: Bohart and Menke (1976), Stizini, pp. 523-531.

Bembicina - 3 Genera

Genera: Bembix, Bicyrtes, Microbembex

CoupletCharacter stateGo to / genus
1aDistal half of marginal cell bends away from the wing margin; propodeal lateral angles do not project; palpi reduced.Microbembex
1bMarginal cell follows the wing margin.2
