Checklist: California Bembix species and Santa Barbara County relevance

Last updated: 2026-07-31

Repository note: this is now a genus-level Bembix checklist inside the broader Bembicidae workspace. For the family-level eastern-Sierra scope, use checklists/bembicidae/eastern-sierra-working-list.md.

Scope

This is a working checklist of California species of Bembix for the insect-identification keys in this repository, with emphasis on taxa relevant to a female specimen from coastal Santa Barbara County or the Dangermond Preserve that has conspicuously spiny forelegs.

The checklist treats species as the primary units and lists named California forms of Bembix americana separately because those subspecies are important for Santa Barbara County and Channel Islands identifications.

Current interpretation

Bohart and Horning (1971) treated 11 California species of Bembix. A search for species described or added after that revision found one additional valid California species, Bembix inyoensis Kimsey and Kimsey, 1981. The same search found one later-described California subspecies, Bembix americana dugi Menke in Rust, Menke, and Miller, 1985, from San Clemente Island.

The resulting working checklist is 12 California Bembix species, plus named California B. americana subspecies. No other post-1971 California Bembix species were found in Pulawski's current catalog or in targeted literature searches on 2026-07-31. Bembix boharti Griswold, 1983 was checked carefully because it is a nearby post-1971 species, but its type series and discussion place it in Baja California, not California.

For a mainland coastal Santa Barbara County or Dangermond Preserve female with conspicuously spiny forelegs, the strongest checklist candidate is Bembix americana comata. If the specimen is from the northern Channel Islands rather than the mainland, Bembix americana hamata becomes important.

Notes on newly described and post-1971 taxa

  • Bembix inyoensis is the only post-1971 species found for California in the current search. The original description was confirmed bibliographically, but a full accessible copy was not available during this pass. The checklist therefore cites Pulawski for type locality and taxonomic status and treats female foreleg characters as not scored until the original description is reviewed.
  • Bembix americana dugi is a post-1971 subspecies, not an added species. It matters for a complete California checklist because it is a named California Channel Islands form from San Clemente Island.
  • Bembix boharti has seven female fore-basitarsal rake setae and is close to B. sayi, but current literature places it outside California.
