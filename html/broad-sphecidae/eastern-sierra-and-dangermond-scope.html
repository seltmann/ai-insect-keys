Working scope: California broad-Sphecidae with Dangermond and Eastern Sierra flags

Last updated: 2026-08-10

Scope

This note records the current working scope for California spheciform wasps in the broad Sphecidae sense: Apoidea excluding bees. It is meant to support future checklist and key work for the Dangermond Preserve, coastal Santa Barbara County, and the Eastern Sierra.

The shared working notes treated broad Sphecidae as a 90-genus California checklist distributed across 10 current family buckets. The user-supplied DOCX files are now represented in companion repository pages for the family key and 90-genera list and the small genus keys by tribe. This document keeps the regional flag definitions and Sierra-priority starting points that still need occurrence-level auditing.

Regional flag legend

Use these flags consistently in later genus and species tables:

FlagMeaning
ExpectedStrong records or strong ecological fit for the region.
LikelyProbably present, but not yet documented locally in the sources checked.
PossibleCalifornia genus with plausible regional occurrence.
UnlikelyCalifornia genus, but poor geographic or habitat fit for the region.

Regional scope:

  • Dangermond means the Jack and Laura Dangermond Preserve and adjacent mainland coastal Santa Barbara County context. Do not transfer Channel Islands-only signals to mainland Dangermond records without specimen or literature evidence.
  • Eastern Sierra means the eastern Sierra Nevada slope plus adjacent Mono and Inyo County valleys, including Owens Valley. Treat desert-floor Inyo records separately from montane Sierra records when habitat matters.

These are the first groups to audit for Eastern Sierra or Dangermond keys because they are already represented in sources reviewed for this repository or are prominent California broad-Sphecidae groups.
